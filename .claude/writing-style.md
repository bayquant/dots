# Writing Style

## Em dashes

Avoid em dashes (—) to separate sentences. Pick the connector that matches what the dash is actually doing:

- If it's just joining two independent clauses, split into two sentences with a period. Usually the cleanest fix.
- If it's introducing an explanation or elaboration, use a colon instead.
- If it's adding a parenthetical aside, use parentheses, or commas if it's short.
- If it's contrasting two related clauses, a semicolon often reads more naturally, or "but"/"so" as a conjunction.
